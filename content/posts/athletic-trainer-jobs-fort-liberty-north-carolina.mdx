## Why Fort Liberty Is One of the Premier H2F Locations for Athletic Trainers

Fort Liberty, located in Cumberland County, North Carolina, stands as one of the most significant military installations in the United States. Spanning more than 160,000 acres, it serves as home to some of the Army's most elite units, including the renowned 82nd Airborne Division, U.S. Army Special Operations Command, and the XVIII Airborne Corps. For athletic trainers seeking meaningful careers in military healthcare, Fort Liberty represents one of the most dynamic and rewarding duty stations available through the Holistic Health and Fitness (H2F) program.

Cognito Systems, a joint venture of Resolution Think and QuarterLine, staffed by PSI, manages athletic trainer positions at Fort Liberty as part of the Army's H2F initiative. These roles offer athletic trainers the opportunity to work alongside some of the most physically demanding units in the military, applying their clinical expertise in a setting that directly impacts operational readiness and the long-term health of service members.

## Understanding the H2F Program at Fort Liberty

The Holistic Health and Fitness program represents the Army's comprehensive approach to soldier performance and readiness. Rather than treating injuries reactively, H2F embeds interdisciplinary performance teams directly into brigade combat teams and other operational units. Athletic trainers serve as core members of these teams, working alongside strength and conditioning coaches, physical therapists, dietitians, and cognitive performance specialists.

At Fort Liberty, the H2F program carries particular significance given the installation's mission profile. Service members assigned here frequently engage in airborne operations, long-range foot movements, and other physically demanding activities that place extraordinary stress on the musculoskeletal system. Athletic trainers working within these units develop a deep understanding of the specific injury patterns and prevention strategies relevant to airborne and special operations forces.

### What Makes Fort Liberty's H2F Program Unique

The sheer scale and diversity of units at Fort Liberty create an environment unlike any other installation. Athletic trainers may find themselves supporting paratroopers preparing for airborne operations one week and working with support units on movement mechanics the next. This variety means that no two days look exactly alike, and the clinical challenges keep even experienced practitioners engaged and growing.

The installation's H2F infrastructure continues to expand, with dedicated performance facilities and training spaces designed specifically for the integrated team approach. Athletic trainers have access to modern equipment, treatment spaces, and the collaborative resources needed to deliver evidence-based care.

## Daily Life as an Athletic Trainer at Fort Liberty

### The Schedule That Sets Military Healthcare Apart

One of the most compelling aspects of athletic trainer positions at Fort Liberty is the schedule. Positions typically follow a Monday through Friday workweek, with shifts running from approximately 6:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. This 40-hour weekly schedule stands in stark contrast to the unpredictable hours that many athletic trainers experience in collegiate or professional sports settings.

There are no Friday night games, no weekend tournaments, and no travel schedules that keep practitioners away from home for weeks at a time. Athletic trainers at Fort Liberty go home every night to their own beds, in their own community, with their evenings and weekends free.

### A Typical Day on the Job

The morning begins early, often aligning with the unit's physical training schedule. Athletic trainers are present during PT sessions, observing movement patterns, providing on-the-spot assessments, and working with service members who may be managing minor musculoskeletal issues. This proactive presence is a cornerstone of the H2F model — rather than waiting for injuries to present at a clinic, athletic trainers identify risk factors and intervene early.

Mid-morning often shifts to individual treatment sessions. Service members with existing injuries or those returning from medical profiles receive hands-on care including therapeutic exercise prescription, manual therapy techniques, taping and bracing, and functional movement assessments. Athletic trainers document their encounters and coordinate with other members of the H2F team to ensure continuity of care.

Afternoons may include educational sessions for service members, team meetings with the broader H2F staff, or program development work. Athletic trainers at Fort Liberty often contribute to injury prevention programs tailored to the specific demands of their assigned unit, drawing on both their clinical training and the data gathered from daily interactions with soldiers.

### The Team-Based Approach

Athletic trainers at Fort Liberty do not work in isolation. The H2F model is built on interdisciplinary collaboration, and each performance team includes professionals from multiple disciplines. This team-based structure means athletic trainers have colleagues to consult with, share observations with, and develop comprehensive care plans alongside.

For practitioners who have worked in settings where they were the sole provider responsible for every aspect of athlete care, the H2F team structure can feel like a significant upgrade. There is time to focus on what athletic trainers do best — musculoskeletal assessment, injury prevention, rehabilitation, and return-to-duty protocols — while knowing that nutrition, mental performance, and strength and conditioning needs are being addressed by equally qualified specialists.

## Qualifications and Requirements

### Education and Certification

Athletic trainer positions at Fort Liberty through Cognito Systems require, at minimum, a bachelor's degree from an accredited athletic training program. A master's degree in athletic training or a related field is preferred and increasingly common among successful applicants. All candidates must hold current certification from the Board of Certification (BOC) and maintain a valid state license to practice athletic training in North Carolina.

### Experience

Candidates should have demonstrated experience in musculoskeletal assessment and rehabilitation. Experience working with physically active populations is particularly valuable given the demands placed on service members at Fort Liberty. Prior military healthcare experience is beneficial but not required — many athletic trainers transition successfully from collegiate, clinical, or sports medicine backgrounds.

### Additional Requirements

Positions require the ability to obtain and maintain appropriate security clearances. A valid driver's license and the ability to pass a background check are standard requirements. CPR and AED certification must be current at the time of hire and maintained throughout employment.

## Living in the Fayetteville, North Carolina Area

### Affordable Cost of Living

The Fayetteville metropolitan area offers one of the more affordable cost-of-living profiles in the eastern United States. Housing costs, in particular, run well below national averages, whether renting or purchasing. Athletic trainers relocating to the area often find that their compensation stretches significantly further than it would in larger metropolitan markets.

Neighborhoods popular with Fort Liberty-affiliated professionals include areas like Southern Pines, Pinehurst, Spring Lake, and various communities within Fayetteville itself. Each offers a different character, from the golf-centric lifestyle of the Pinehurst area to the more suburban feel of northwest Fayetteville.

### Climate and Geography

North Carolina's Sandhills region enjoys a moderate climate with four distinct seasons. Winters are mild compared to northern states, with average January temperatures in the mid-40s Fahrenheit. Summers are warm and humid, typical of the southeastern United States. The region sees roughly 210 sunny days per year, providing ample opportunity for outdoor activities throughout most of the calendar.

### Proximity to Major Destinations

Fort Liberty's central North Carolina location places it within reasonable driving distance of several appealing destinations. Raleigh, the state capital and a thriving tech hub, sits approximately 70 miles to the northeast. The beaches of the North Carolina coast are roughly three hours to the east, while the Blue Ridge Mountains are a similar distance to the west.

Charlotte, one of the fastest-growing cities in the Southeast, is about two hours southwest. This geographic positioning gives Fort Liberty-area residents access to mountain weekends, beach trips, and big-city amenities without requiring a plane ticket.

### Community and Culture

Fayetteville has a deep connection to the military community, and that connection creates a support network that can make relocation easier. The city offers a growing dining scene, local arts venues, outdoor markets, and community events throughout the year. The Cape Fear River Trail provides miles of walking and cycling paths, and several state parks and natural areas are within easy reach.

For athletic trainers with families, the area offers a range of school options and family-friendly neighborhoods. The community's familiarity with the military lifestyle means that neighbors, businesses, and local organizations understand and support the unique rhythms of installation-adjacent life.
