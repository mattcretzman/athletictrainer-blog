# Athletic Trainer Jobs in Texas: Military Base Positions at Fort Cavazos, Fort Bliss & More

Texas is home to more military installations than any other state in the country, making it one of the most active markets for certified athletic trainers seeking careers in military healthcare. From the sprawling armored training grounds of Fort Cavazos in Central Texas to the desert ranges of Fort Bliss in El Paso and the medical hub of Fort Sam Houston in San Antonio, the Lone Star State offers a range of professional environments, climates, and lifestyle options for athletic trainers.

Cognito Systems, a joint venture of Resolution Think and QuarterLine, staffed by PSI, connects certified athletic trainers with positions at military installations across Texas. These roles are primarily filled through the Army's Holistic Health and Fitness (H2F) program and defense contractor agreements, offering predictable schedules, team-based practice, and direct impact on service member readiness.

## Why Texas Is a Top State for Military Athletic Trainers

### Unmatched Concentration of Military Installations

Texas hosts more than 15 military installations across all branches of service. The state's vast geography, favorable weather for year-round training, and deep military heritage have made it a natural center of gravity for the Department of Defense. For athletic trainers, this concentration translates into a broader selection of positions, more frequent openings, and greater flexibility in choosing a location that fits personal and professional goals.

### No State Income Tax

Texas is one of a handful of states that does not levy a personal income tax. For athletic trainers comparing offers across multiple states, this is a meaningful financial consideration. Competitive compensation paired with no state income tax can make Texas positions particularly attractive from a net earnings perspective.

### Cost of Living Advantages

Outside of the major metro cores of Austin and Dallas, much of Texas offers a cost of living that falls below the national average. Cities near military installations — including Killeen, El Paso, and San Antonio — provide affordable housing, reasonable commute times, and access to amenities without the price pressures found in coastal markets. This makes it possible to build long-term financial stability while working in a mission-driven career.

### Texas Athletic Trainer Licensure

Texas regulates athletic trainers through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Athletic trainers must hold a current BOC certification and obtain a Texas license to practice. The application process is straightforward, and TDLR processes applications relatively quickly compared to some other states. For athletic trainers relocating from out of state, Texas does not impose unusually burdensome additional requirements beyond BOC certification and standard background checks.

## Fort Cavazos (Killeen, Texas)

### Installation Overview

Fort Cavazos — formerly Fort Hood — is the largest active-duty armored military installation in the world. Located in Central Texas between Killeen and Copperas Cove, the installation spans approximately 214,000 acres and is home to III Corps headquarters, the 1st Cavalry Division, and numerous supporting units. With more than 45,000 active-duty service members, Fort Cavazos generates significant and sustained demand for athletic trainers.

### Athletic Trainer Opportunities

The H2F program at Fort Cavazos supports multiple Brigade Combat Teams, each requiring a full interdisciplinary performance team that includes athletic trainers. These roles place athletic trainers directly within units, working alongside strength and conditioning coaches, physical therapists, registered dietitians, and cognitive performance specialists. Additional clinical opportunities exist at Darnall Army Medical Center.

Athletic trainers at Fort Cavazos work Monday through Friday daytime hours, with 40-hour work weeks and no on-call requirements. The work is autonomous — athletic trainers conduct their own evaluations, develop treatment plans, and manage return-to-duty progressions without the overhead of a traditional clinical billing model.

### Living in the Killeen-Temple Area

The Killeen-Temple-Fort Cavazos metropolitan area has a population of approximately 475,000. Housing costs sit well below the national average, making homeownership accessible on a military healthcare salary. Austin is roughly 60 miles to the south, offering access to a major cultural and entertainment hub for weekend trips. The area's climate is warm, with hot summers and mild winters that allow for year-round outdoor activity.

## Fort Bliss (El Paso, Texas)

### Installation Overview

Fort Bliss occupies more than 1.12 million acres in far west Texas and southern New Mexico, making it one of the largest military installations in the United States by land area. The installation is home to the 1st Armored Division and serves as a premier training ground for armored, air defense, and joint operations. The combination of high-altitude desert terrain, intense sun exposure, and extreme temperature ranges creates unique challenges for service member health and performance — and unique professional opportunities for athletic trainers.

### Athletic Trainer Opportunities

H2F performance teams at Fort Bliss integrate athletic trainers into brigade-level operations, where the physical demands of desert training, altitude acclimatization, and heat-related injury prevention define the daily caseload. Athletic trainers here work closely with service members preparing for deployments to arid environments, making heat illness prevention, hydration protocols, and load carriage optimization core competencies.

The schedule follows the same predictable Monday through Friday, 40-hour structure found at other H2F installations. Athletic trainers are home every night and maintain consistent schedules that support personal and family life.

### Living in El Paso

El Paso is a city of approximately 680,000 residents, situated on the U.S.-Mexico border with a distinctive Southwestern culture and bilingual community character. The cost of living is among the lowest of any major U.S. city, with housing costs significantly below national averages. El Paso offers over 300 days of sunshine per year, access to Franklin Mountains State Park for hiking and mountain biking, and a rich food and arts scene influenced by its cross-border heritage. For outdoor enthusiasts and those who enjoy warm, dry climates, El Paso is an appealing home base.

For the full guide to positions at Fort Bliss, see the [Fort Bliss athletic trainer jobs guide](/blog/athletic-trainer-jobs-fort-bliss-texas).

## Fort Sam Houston (San Antonio, Texas)

### Installation Overview

Fort Sam Houston, part of Joint Base San Antonio (JBSA), serves as the headquarters for U.S. Army South and the Army Medical Center of Excellence. San Antonio has been called "Military City, USA" for good reason — the broader JBSA complex, which also includes Lackland Air Force Base and Randolph Air Force Base, represents one of the largest concentrations of military personnel and medical training infrastructure in the country.

### Athletic Trainer Opportunities

Fort Sam Houston's role as the Army's medical training hub creates a unique environment for athletic trainers. While H2F positions may be available as the program continues to expand across installations, the San Antonio military community also supports athletic training roles connected to medical readiness training, rehabilitation programs, and the extensive military treatment facility network at Brooke Army Medical Center (BAMC).

Athletic trainers at JBSA may work in both clinical and field-based settings, depending on the specific contract and program assignment. The interdisciplinary exposure is significant — working alongside military medical trainees, physicians, and allied health professionals in one of the most advanced military medical centers in the world.

### Living in San Antonio

San Antonio is a city of approximately 1.5 million residents (over 2.5 million in the metro area), offering the amenities of a major city with a cost of living that remains moderate for its size. The city is known for the Riverwalk, a thriving food scene, professional sports, and a deeply rooted military community culture. San Antonio's warm climate supports year-round outdoor activity, and the city's size provides robust options for housing, entertainment, dining, and recreation. For athletic trainers with families, the school systems in suburban areas north and west of the city receive consistently strong rankings.

## What to Expect Working as an Athletic Trainer in Texas

### Schedule and Work-Life Balance

Athletic trainers in Texas military healthcare positions typically work Monday through Friday daytime hours, with 40-hour weeks. There are no on-call requirements, no weekend shifts, and no evening games or events to attend. This schedule stands in stark contrast to the demands of collegiate or professional sports settings, where 60-hour weeks, travel, and weekend commitments are the norm.

The early-afternoon finish time is particularly valuable in Texas, where late afternoon temperatures during summer months can be intense. Athletic trainers have their evenings free for family, recreation, continuing education, or simply enjoying the long Texas daylight hours.

### Scope of Practice and Professional Autonomy

Military athletic trainer positions in Texas allow for a broad scope of practice. Athletic trainers conduct independent evaluations, provide hands-on treatment, design and implement injury prevention programs, and make return-to-duty decisions in collaboration with the medical team. The emphasis is on keeping service members healthy and mission-ready — not on generating billable patient encounters.

### Team-Based Environment

H2F performance teams bring together athletic trainers, strength and conditioning coaches, physical therapists, registered dietitians, occupational therapists, and cognitive performance specialists. This interdisciplinary model fosters professional growth, reduces isolation, and creates a collaborative environment where athletic trainers are valued as essential team members rather than ancillary staff.

## How to Apply for Athletic Trainer Jobs in Texas

### Requirements

- Current BOC certification
- Texas TDLR athletic trainer license (or eligibility to obtain one)
- Master's degree from a CAATE-accredited program (for positions requiring it)
- Eligibility for a Department of Defense security clearance
- U.S. citizenship (required for most government contract positions)

### The Application Process

Cognito Systems works with certified athletic trainers to match qualifications and preferences with available positions across Texas installations. The process includes credential verification, security clearance initiation, and placement coordination. Because Texas has multiple active installations, candidates may have the flexibility to indicate geographic preferences during the application process.

### Timeline Considerations

Security clearance processing can take several weeks to several months, depending on the type of clearance required and the candidate's background. Starting the process early — even before a specific position is confirmed — can help reduce wait times and speed placement.
