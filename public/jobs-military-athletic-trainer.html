Military Athletic Trainer Jobs

Certified Athletic Trainers are working inside military performance centers right now. Same credential you already have. No enlistment. No military experience required. You are a civilian contractor.

What Military AT Looks Like

You Are a Civilian

No boot camp. No uniform. No enlistment. You show up as a BOC-certified Athletic Trainer and do what you were trained to do. The military provides the mission. You provide the care.

Consistent Schedule

Monday through Friday, daytime hours. No weekends. No on-call. No 60-hour weeks during football season. ATs who make the switch say this is the biggest difference.

Real Team Support

You are never "one deep." Work alongside PTs, dietitians, strength coaches, and psychologists. One AT covering 2,700 students at a high school? That does not happen here.

Locations Across the Country and Overseas

Fort Liberty (NC), Fort Cavazos (TX), Fort Bliss (TX), Fort Drum (NY), JBLM (WA), Camp Pendleton (CA), Hawaii, Alaska, Germany, Japan, South Korea, and more.
