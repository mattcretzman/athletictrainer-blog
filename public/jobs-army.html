Athletic Trainer Jobs with the Army

The U.S. Army is hiring civilian Athletic Trainers through Cognito Systems. Embedded support for soldiers at Army installations across the country and overseas. Same BOC credential. Entirely different career.

Why ATs Choose the Army

No Enlistment Required

You are a civilian contractor. No boot camp, no uniform, no military obligation. You bring your BOC certification and your clinical skills. The Army provides the mission and the infrastructure.

Work at Major Army Bases

Fort Liberty (NC), Fort Cavazos (TX), Fort Bliss (TX), Fort Drum (NY), Fort Campbell (KY), Fort Riley (KS), Fort Moore (GA), Fort Stewart (GA), and more. Positions at 25+ Army installations.

Impact at Scale

The Army's H2F program reduced injuries by 33%. You are not taping ankles on a sideline. You are running injury prevention programs, movement screenings, and return-to-duty protocols for active duty soldiers.

Your Career Grows With You

Move between bases as your life changes. Hawaii, Alaska, Germany, Japan, Korea. The Army has positions worldwide, and your contract moves with you.

- David Zhang, H2F Program Manager
